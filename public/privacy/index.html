Privacy Policy

Last updated Sep 27, 2026

This policy explains what information TimeLoom (“the site”, “we”) collects and how it is used.

Information you enter

The locations, times and settings you choose in the planner are processed in your browser. Your saved locations and last plan are stored in your browser's local storage so they are there on your next visit. They are not sent to us. You can remove them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

A share link contains the plan itself (the locations, date, time and clock format). Anyone you send the link to can see that plan.

Server logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard request logs, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. These logs are used to keep the site running and secure.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.

For more on how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time through the privacy settings link shown with the consent message.

Fonts

The site loads the Inter font from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's servers to download it.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
