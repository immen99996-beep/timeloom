About

TimeLoom is an independent project built and run by Ho. It is made for anyone who plans calls or deadlines with people in other countries and needs to check local time, weekends and public holidays on every side at once.

What the site does

  • Converts one moment into local time for every location you add, including the date and UTC offset.
  • Marks each location's day as a working day, weekend or public holiday, using that country's own weekend.
  • Finds the hours when everyone's working day overlaps.
  • Lists national public holidays for 246 countries and territories.

Where the data comes from

Time zones come from the IANA time zone database. The planner uses the copy built into your browser, so daylight saving changes are applied for the date you pick.

Public holidays come from the open-source python-holidays project, which is maintained by volunteers and checked against official government sources. The site includes national holidays only. Regional holidays, bank holidays and one-off days announced at short notice may be missing.

Flags come from the open-source flag-icons set.

Accuracy

Holiday dates are checked when the data is updated, but governments sometimes add or move holidays during the year. For anything important, such as a contract deadline or a flight, confirm the date with an official source. If you find a mistake, please let us know.
